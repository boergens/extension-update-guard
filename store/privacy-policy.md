# Update Guard privacy policy

Update Guard does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It makes no network requests.

To do its job, Update Guard reads the list of extensions installed in your browser (name, version,
description, permissions, homepage and update address) through Chrome's `management` API. It stores the
following in the browser's local extension storage on your device:

- the last version of each extension that you approved,
- the updates that are waiting for your review,
- your settings,
- a history of up to 200 events (which extension updated, when, and what you decided).

This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you remove Update Guard from Chrome.
